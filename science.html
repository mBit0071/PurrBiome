Gut Health = Whole Health

Why the Feline Microbiome Matters

Just like in humans, a cat's gut is home to trillions of microorganisms — bacteria, fungi, and viruses — that play a critical role in overall health. This complex ecosystem, known as the gut microbiome, influences everything from digestion and nutrient absorption to immune function and even mood regulation.

When the microbiome falls out of balance — a condition called dysbiosis — it can lead to chronic inflammation, digestive issues, skin problems, and a weakened immune system. Over time, dysbiosis has been linked to reduced lifespan and quality of life in felines.

  • Mood & Behavior — The gut-brain axis means a healthy gut supports a calm, happy cat.
  • Digestion & Weight — Balanced microbiota optimise nutrient extraction and metabolism.
  • Longevity — Lower chronic inflammation correlates with a longer, healthier life.
