How PurrBiome Works

From sample collection to personalized insights — here's how pet parents use PurrBiome to take control of their cat's health journey.

Step 1
1 Collect & Test

Gather a simple sample at home

Using our easy-to-follow at-home collection kit, you'll gather a small fecal sample from your cat — no vet visit required. The process takes less than 5 minutes and is completely stress-free for both you and your feline friend.

Once collected, you mail the sample to our partnered laboratory in the pre-paid packaging. Results are typically available within 7–10 business days.

  • No prescription needed
  • Safe, non-invasive process
  • Pre-paid return shipping included
2 Analyze in App

Explore rich visualizations of your cat's microbiome

When results are ready, you'll receive a notification. Log into the PurrBiome dashboard to explore interactive charts, diversity scores, and biomarker comparisons. Every metric is presented in plain language with clear benchmarks.

Our AI engine cross-references your cat's data against a growing database of feline health profiles to highlight what's normal, what's notable, and what might need attention.

  • Interactive microbiome visualizations
  • Benchmarking against breed & age averages
  • AI-powered trend detection

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with PurrBiome.

How often should I test my cat? +

We recommend an initial baseline test followed by a retest every 3–6 months to track changes over time. Cats with known digestive issues or those on a new diet may benefit from more frequent testing. The dashboard will remind you when it's time for a follow-up.

Is this meant to replace my vet? +

Absolutely not. PurrBiome is a complementary tool designed to empower you with data you can share with your veterinarian. We help you spot trends early, but always consult your vet for medical diagnoses, treatment plans, or urgent health concerns. Think of PurrBiome as your cat's wellness co-pilot.

How do I access the interactive demo? +

You can access the full interactive demo right now by visiting PurrBiome.streamlit.app. No sign-up required — explore real microbiome visualizations, simulate health scores, and experience the PurrBiome platform firsthand.
